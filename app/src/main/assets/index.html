How the numbers work
  • Agent SIM — Cash In: cash in hand goes up, that Agent SIM balance goes down.
  • Agent SIM — Cash Out: cash in hand goes down, that Agent SIM balance goes up.
  • Personal / Merchant SIM — Cash In: money comes into that SIM, so cash in hand goes down and the SIM balance goes up.
  • Personal / Merchant SIM — Cash Out: money leaves that SIM, so cash in hand goes up and the SIM balance goes down.
  • Commission is filled automatically from the rate per 1000 (bKash Agent 4 in / 3.75 out, Nagad Agent 4.10 both). You can type your own number any time. By default it is added to that SIM balance.
  • Profit = sales profit + commission − expense. Buying float or adding cash never counts as profit.
  • Due sale: profit is counted, but no cash is added. The amount goes into the Baki tab under that customer's name. When they pay, record it there as "Taka pelam".

How baki khata works
  • Every customer or supplier gets their own khata page. Tap a name to open it.
  • Dilam / Becha — I gave goods, service or cash. Their baki goes up, so I will get more.
  • Pelam — I got money back. Their baki goes down.
  • If the balance turns the other way the app shows I will pay, so a supplier khata works with the same two buttons.
  • Tap any old line to edit or delete it — the balance fixes itself right away.
  • Pick "Goods / service" when no cash moved, or Cash / bKash / Nagad when real money moved, so cash in hand and SIM balances stay correct.
  • Sales tab: payment Due (not paid) + customer name → the entry lands here by itself.
